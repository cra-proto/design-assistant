# Canada.ca Style

Use this skill when a parent workflow writes or rewrites Canada.ca content.

## Workflow
1. Read `references/writing-rules.json` for reusable Canada.ca writing rules.
2. Apply rules that match the parent workflow's content type and output contract.
3. Prefer direct, plain-language wording that helps people complete tasks.
4. Preserve meaning, eligibility, deadlines, legal requirements, service details, and policy nuance.

## Resources
- Read `references/writing-rules.json` for general writing rules.

## Output Boundary
- Do not change the parent workflow's output format.
- Do not invent policy details, deadlines, eligibility criteria, service standards, contact channels, or destination details.
- Do not claim that content passed a full QA review unless the parent workflow explicitly requires a QA pass.
